# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is trying to help.

## What that means here

Good: asking questions, disagreeing with an idea, saying you do not understand something, changing
your mind, saying no to a feature and explaining why.

Not good: personal attacks, harassment in public or private, sexual attention or imagery, posting
someone's private information, or dismissing a person rather than an argument.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/czearing). Reports stay
private. You will get a reply within a few days.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, or block accounts that do not follow this,
and will explain the decision when asked.
